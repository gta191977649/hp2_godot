Read-only Ghidra MCP decompilation calls `GetJoystickType` and returns true for
type bytes `0x53`, `0x73` and `0x79`. `JoyHandler_Throttle` at `0013B7B0`
passes the driver's stored port and applies a saturating fourfold multiplier
to its primary analog channel when the predicate succeeds.

The Godot-free host binding is in `src/game_core/src/joysticke.cpp`.
Physical device discovery and calibration are separate host boundaries.
