## Confirmed findings

Generates/caches texture packets using texture record+0x98. Writes TEX0_1,
TEX1_1, ALPHA_1, MIPTBP1_1, MIPTBP2_1 and CLAMP_1, plus coordinate data.
TCC is forced1; TFX comes from byte+0x4E. ALPHA low selectors come from
byte+0x76 and FIX from byte+0x77. TEX1 uses bytes+0x6E..0x71 and signed16+0x72.
CLAMP derives WMS/WMT from inverted bits0/1 of byte+0x4C unless a global
override is active. See `reverse/structures/vehicle-gs-texture-state-task0033.md`
for original asset values, exact supported equations, validation and limits.
