## Structural guarantees and limitations

The checker first reconstructs the historical/frozen TASK-0044/0045 chain up to the
pre-TASK-0046 inventory, then requires an exact before/after permission for every
production function change, addition or removal. Paths and signatures are checked,
including new throwing gaps. Negative fixtures reject drift and missing predecessors.
Canonical-owner registration is a separate version-scoped successor, preventing
generation of duplicate noarg templates without deleting historical Retail records.

This does not certify PS2 floating-point, controller hardware, whole-world startup,
full Clan topology, DMA/raster behavior or original numerical equivalence. No Verified
promotion is authorized by a source review or a passing PC/real-window test.
